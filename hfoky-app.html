Hfoky App

A simple way to estimate how much you earn from buying and reselling items.

Know your profit on every flip

Hfoky App is a concept for a practical profit calculator for people who buy phones and resell them. Add what you paid, what you sold an item for, and any extra costs—such as shipping, repairs, or platform fees—to estimate your profit.

The idea is to make it easier to keep each deal organized, see which flips are worth your time, and understand your results over time.

  • Record purchase and selling prices.
  • Include additional costs that affect your earnings.
  • See an estimated profit for each item.
