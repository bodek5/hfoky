EMUI to HarmonyOS conversion tutorial!

This is a quick tutorial on how to convert an EMUI device to HarmonyOS using KirinTool.

Follow the steps below to get started:

1. Install the official free Kirin-Tool and the Huawei COM 1.0 drivers.

2. Open the phone and boot it into testpoint mode.

3. In Kirin-Tool, select your processor and click "Unlock Fastboot"; ensure the battery remains connected if you need to unplug the device from the cable.

4. After unlocking fastboot, go to the "OEM Info" tab and switch to the Chinese variant (e.g., change VOG-L29 to VOG-AL00 for the P30 Pro, and set the lower option to "all/cn").

5. Go to the "FLASH" tab and flash the DLOAD files for your device.

6. To ensure safety and a successful boot, go to "Security Tools" and click "Remove FRP"; the phone will then wipe itself second time and boot into HarmonyOS or the Chinese version of EMUI, which can be updated to a newer version of HarmonyOS.

NOTE: If your phone does not detect an update, you must find a newer DLOAD file; if a verification error occurs, you need to flash a different DLOAD file.
